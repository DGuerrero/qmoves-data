# Rutalo privacy policy (iOS)

[Leer en español](PRIVACY-iOS-es.md)

**Last updated:** 9 November 2026

Rutalo is a public transport app for Bilbao and Bizkaia. It is designed to work without collecting
information about you.

## What we collect

Nothing. Rutalo has no account, advertising, analytics, or crash reporting, and we run no server that
receives information from the app. We do not create a profile or keep a location history.

## Location

If you allow it, Rutalo uses your location to show the stops around you, list nearby departures, and
start trips from where you are. Location is processed only on your device: route planning runs on
the phone and your position is not sent to us or to anyone else. You can use the app without
location access, starting from the map instead.

## Saved stops

Saved stops and your Home and Work places are stored only on your device, and are never sent to us. They are included in your
device's own iCloud or computer backups, under your Apple account, if you use those. Deleting the app
deletes them from the device.

## Display preferences

Your appearance, map style and network filter choices are stored only on your device.

## Network requests

Rutalo downloads public data directly from these providers. Your request does not contain your
location, saved stops, or any identifier of ours, but like any internet request it shows the provider
your IP address and technical details such as the device's network software, under their own terms.

| Provider | What is requested | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GitHub | List of cities, and the city's timetable package and its version file | At launch |
| OpenFreeMap | Map style and map tiles for the area on screen; the whole city's map when you save it for offline use | While the map is shown; the offline map only when you save it in Settings |
| Open Data Euskadi | Live times for Metro Bilbao, Bizkaibus and Euskotren; vehicle positions for these and Bilbobus; their service alerts | Live times while departures for them are on screen; vehicle positions every 30 seconds while a stop is open; alerts every few minutes while the app is open |
| Renfe | Live times, train positions and service alerts for Renfe Cercanías | Live times while departures for it are on screen; train positions every 30 seconds while a stop is open; alerts every few minutes while the app is open |

The app makes these requests only while it is open. The map tiles you view show that provider which
area you are looking at.

The About screen's links open in your browser, under those sites' own policies.

## Children

Rutalo collects no data from anyone, including children.
