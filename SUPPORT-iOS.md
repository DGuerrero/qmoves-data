# Rutalo support (iOS)

Rutalo is a public transport app for Bilbao and Bizkaia. It is an independent app, not affiliated with the
transport operators.

Questions, problems or ideas: [david@quodersmobile.com](mailto:david@quodersmobile.com)

It helps to include your iPhone model, your iOS version, and the line or stop involved.

**Times look wrong?** Times marked with a green dot come live from the operators, and others are timetable times.
Operators sometimes change services at short notice, so check their notices for anything urgent.

**Stops around you aren't showing?** Rutalo needs location access for this. Turn it on in the app's Settings tab,
or start from the map instead.
