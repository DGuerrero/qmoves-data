# QMoves privacy policy

*Last updated: 20 October 2026*

QMoves is a public transport app for Bilbao and Bizkaia. It is designed to work without collecting
anything about you.

## What we collect

Nothing. QMoves has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting. We do not run
any server that receives data from the app.

## Your location

If you allow it, QMoves uses your device's location to show stops near you and to plan trips from
where you are. The location is used only on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else,
and it is not stored after you close the app. You can use QMoves without location access by
searching for places or picking a point on the map.

On Android, location is provided by Google Play services. Google's handling of that data is covered
by [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Favourites

Stops you save as favourites are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its
data deletes them.

## Network requests the app makes

To work, QMoves downloads public data. These requests contain no personal data, but as with any
internet request the server sees your IP address and basic technical details:

| What | From | When |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Timetables, stops and places | GitHub (github.com) | First launch, then when a newer version exists |
| Live departure times | Basque Government open data (opendata.euskadi.eus) and Renfe (gtfsrt.renfe.com) | While you look at departures or trips |
| Map tiles | OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org) | While the map is on screen |

Map tiles are requested for the area shown on the map, which may be the area around you. No
identifier or location coordinates are sent with any request.

## Children

QMoves is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.
