# Rutalo privacy policy

*Last updated: 25 October 2026*

Rutalo is a public transport app for Bilbao and Bizkaia. It is designed to collect as little as
possible: no account, no advertising, and nothing that identifies you.

## What we collect

Only two things, both through Google Firebase, and both of which you control in **Settings →
Privacy**:

**Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics).** If the app crashes, it sends a report so we can fix the
problem: what went wrong in the code, the app version, the phone model and Android version, free
memory and storage at the time, and a random installation identifier created by Crashlytics. Crash
reports are on by default; you can turn them off at any time. Reports are kept for 90 days.

**Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase), only if you agree.** The first time you use
Rutalo we ask whether you want to share anonymous usage statistics. If you say yes, the app sends
which screens you open and which features you use (for example "planned a trip from a shortcut",
"opened a line's timetable"), together with the app version, phone model, Android version, language,
approximate location (city or region), which Google works out from the IP address without keeping
the address itself, and a random app-instance identifier. If you say
no, nothing is sent. You can change your answer at any time in Settings; turning statistics off
also deletes what was gathered on the phone and resets the identifier. Statistics are kept for
2 months.

We never send your location, the places or stops you search for, your home or work places, or your
favourites. Advertising identifiers are not collected, and advertising features are switched off.
We do not sell data or share it with anyone else. Google processes this data on our behalf as
described in [Firebase's privacy information](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

We do not run any server of our own that receives data from the app.

## Your location

If you allow it, Rutalo uses your device's location to show stops near you and to plan trips from
where you are. The location is used only on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else,
and it is not stored after you close the app. You can use Rutalo without location access by
searching for places or picking a point on the map.

On Android, location is provided by Google Play services. Google's handling of that data is covered
by [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Favourites and saved places

Stops you save as favourites, and the places you set as home and work, are stored only on your
device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

## Network requests the app makes

To work, Rutalo downloads public data. These requests contain no personal data, but as with any
internet request the server sees your IP address and basic technical details:

| What | From | When |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Timetables, stops and places | GitHub (github.com) | First launch, then when a newer version exists |
| Live departure times | Basque Government open data (opendata.euskadi.eus) and Renfe (gtfsrt.renfe.com) | While you look at departures or trips |
| Map tiles | OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org) | While the map is on screen |
| Crash reports and, if you agreed, usage statistics | Google Firebase | As described above |

Map tiles are requested for the area shown on the map, which may be the area around you. No
location coordinates are sent with any request, and none except the Firebase ones carry an
identifier.

## Children

Rutalo is suitable for all ages. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly
collect personal data from them.

## Your rights
